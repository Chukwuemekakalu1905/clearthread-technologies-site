FAQ

Common questions

How long does a site take?

Most one-page sites are ready in about two weeks. Multi-page sites usually take a little longer, depending on photos and copy.

Do I need to buy a domain?

No. Every plan includes a free subdomain. If you want your own domain, we can add it for $5 a month, or move you to one later.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, updates you request, security maintenance, a simple analytics report, and Google Business Profile help on the plans that include it.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can rebuild a site that is outdated, broken, or hard to update, and then look after it month to month.

Where are you based?

We work with businesses in the Phoenix metro — Phoenix, Tempe, Mesa, Glendale, Goodyear, Gilbert, and nearby cities.
